IEEE ENET’Com SB Blood Donation Campaign
The blood donation campaign was successfully organized through a collaborative effort between all the units of the IEEE ENET'Com Student Branch.

This event reflected the spirit of teamwork, solidarity, and social responsibility shared among the members. Every unit contributed its efforts and resources to ensure the success of the campaign, from organization and communication to logistics and volunteer coordination.

We were also grateful for the participation of all the donors, whose contribution reflected generosity and solidarity by offering a part of themselves to bring hope and help to others.
