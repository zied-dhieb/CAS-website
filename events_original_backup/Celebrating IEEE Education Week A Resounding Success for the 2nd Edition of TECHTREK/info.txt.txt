TECHTREK 2026: Empowering Educators and Inspiring the Next Generation of Innovators

In celebration of IEEE Education Week, the IEEE ENETCOM Student Branch joined forces with the IEEE CASS, RAS, and EMBS ENETCOM Student Branch Chapters, as well as the IEEE WIE ENETCOM Affinity Group. Together, in collaboration with ENET’Com University, JCI Sfax Sekiet Eddair, and the Regional Commission for Education – Sfax 2 , they proudly hosted the second edition of TECHTREK. Dedicated to educators and pre-university students, this landmark event took place on April 12, 2026, at the ENET’Com campus.

The proceedings commenced at 9:00 AM with registration and an opening ceremony, setting the stage for a day of enlightenment and engagement.

Empowering Educators

The teachers’ program began with a compelling keynote address delivered by Miss Jallouli Dorsaf. Her presentation focused on Artificial Intelligence tools in education, demonstrating how technology can facilitate and enhance the teaching process. The session was met with remarkable enthusiasm; the educators actively interacted, posing insightful questions to satisfy their curiosity.

Following the keynote, a coffee break provided a valuable opportunity for networking and informal discussion. Re-energized, the participants reconvened for a panel discussion titled "AI in Education." The session featured three distinguished speakers from diverse backgrounds: a school director with a foundational career in teaching, an engineer, and a final-year university student. Each speaker shared unique insights and professional perspectives on the future of AI in the educational sector, thoughtfully addressing the teachers' inquiries.

Inspiring the Next Generation

Simultaneously, the young students embarked on their own journey of discovery. They began with an introductory AI session that explained the mechanics, applications, and significance of Artificial Intelligence, successfully capturing the imaginations of these young minds.

This was followed by a workshop on public speaking, conducted by JCI. Designed for these "young heroes," the session aimed to boost confidence and teach them how to project their voices effectively to the world. Afterward, the students enjoyed a mini-brunch to recharge for the activities ahead.

The final educational session for the students was a "Sensora" workshop, offering hands-on experience to explore the fascinating world of sensors. As the day progressed, the budding innovators were ready for action. The event culminated in a series of six distinct challenges, ranging from AI image generation to circuit repair. The excitement peaked with a surprise challenge: a mini robotic competition featuring a functional maquette.

A Memorable Conclusion

As TECHTREK drew to a close, the atmosphere was filled with smiles and laughter. The students returned home with new knowledge in their minds and gifts in their hands. Meanwhile, the educators expressed profound gratitude for the opportunity and eagerly requested future collaborations.

We extend our heartfelt thanks to everyone who contributed to the resounding success of this second edition of TECHTREK.
