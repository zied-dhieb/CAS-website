In collaboration with the IEEE CAS ENET'Com SBC, the third session of our robotics bootcamp marked an electrifying milestone for our new recruits! Through an immersive, hands-on workshop, the team officially brought their brand-new line-following robots to life. From meticulously wiring electronic components and compiling complex code to putting their custom creations to the ultimate test on a real track mockup, our members experienced the pure thrill of turning abstract theory into a fully functional machine.

True to our chapter's core philosophy, the atmosphere was a perfect synergy of rigorous engineering and absolute fun ,proving that the best way to master robotics is by enjoying the process. What truly elevated this session to the next level was the immense educational value it delivered. The practical, hands-on nature of the workshop ensured a profound learning experience for everyone involved, sparking creativity and reinforcing a powerful sense of community growth. It was a truly memorable session where technical innovation met genuine connection!
