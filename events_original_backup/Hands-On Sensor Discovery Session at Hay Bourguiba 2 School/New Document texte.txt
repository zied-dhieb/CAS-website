As part of the IEEE Educational Week, the IEEE ENET’Com Student Branch, in collaboration with its CAS SBC and WIE SAG, organized an educational visit to the Hay Bourguiba 2 primary school. This initiative aimed to introduce young students to basic technological concepts in an interactive and engaging manner.

The session was led by Mr. Soulaiman Baklouti, a first-year Industrial Computer Engineering student known for his enthusiasm and passion for technology. He began by introducing himself and interacting with the children to create a friendly and comfortable learning environment.

He then delivered a simplified and well-structured presentation on sensors, where he clearly defined the concept and highlighted different types of sensors, along with their real-world applications. To enhance understanding, he explained how these sensors function in practical scenarios.

To reinforce learning and ensure better retention, the session included a hands-on activity. The speaker brought real sensors and demonstrated their operation to the students in small groups, allowing each child to observe and interact directly with the devices.

Throughout the session, members of JCI, alongside IEEE volunteers, actively guided the children, ensuring smooth organization and maintaining an engaging atmosphere.

The event concluded on a positive note, with all participants having gained practical exposure to sensors. A group photo was taken to commemorate this enriching experience.
