IEEE GATEWAY: Unlocking Opportunities in Engineering Education

As part of IEEE Education Week 2026, the IEEE GATEWAY event at ENET'Com officially launched the EduVision.tn initiative. This impactful gathering, driven by a strong collaboration between four student chapters :RAS, WIE, CAS, and EMBS and WIE SAG, brought together technology enthusiasts to inspire students and unlock new opportunities within the IEEE network.

The session opened with a highly impactful keynote, "Empowering Engineering Education through IEEE," by Mr. Ayoub Kallel, Chair of the IEEE ENET'Com Student Branch. Acting as a true catalyst, he demystified the vast IEEE ecosystem, transforming passive interest into proactive engagement. He demonstrated how future innovators can leverage this global network as a career launchpad, highlighting key platforms:

IEEE Xplore: For accessing world-class technical literature.

IEEE Collabratec: For global networking and mentorship.

IEEE eLearning: For mastering emerging tech and continuous education.

IEEE ResumeLab: For building strong professional portfolios.

By emphasizing student involvement, he empowered the audience, leaving them with a clear, actionable roadmap and a renewed drive to maximize their membership.

Following this, Mr. Bilel Mezghani, TRSYP 2.0 Chair and ENIS PhD Student, delivered a captivating technical session titled "Introduction to Reinforcement Learning." He expertly translated complex AI mechanics—such as agents, environments, and reward signals into highly accessible concepts. Showcasing cutting-edge applications like autonomous robotics and advanced gaming AI, he bridged the gap between research and industry, sparking immense curiosity and leaving attendees highly motivated.

Fostering a highly interactive atmosphere, the event went beyond traditional lectures with a lively Kahoot! competition. This allowed participants to test their newly acquired knowledge, sparking enthusiastic interaction and proving the sessions were both deeply informative and genuinely enjoyable.

Ultimately, IEEE GATEWAY was a resounding success and a powerful kick-off for Education Week. By seamlessly blending leadership insights with high-level technical expertise, we celebrate the outstanding quality of the presentations and the remarkable enthusiasm of the students in  attendance.
