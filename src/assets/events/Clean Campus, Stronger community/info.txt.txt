category: Humanitarian
IEEE ENET'Com SB, in collaboration with its Student Branch Chapters(IAS, IES-PES, CASS, CS, EMBS, and RAS), WIE Affinity Group, and fellow ENET'Com student clubs, organized a campus cleanup initiative at the National School of Electronics and Telecommunications of Sfax. The activity brought students together to contribute to a cleaner and more organized university environment. 

Through collective cleaning and organization efforts, participants contributed to improving the campus environment by cleaning classrooms, organizing common spaces, and arranging club equipment and supplies. Beyond enhancing the school’s facilities, the initiative strengthened teamwork, encouraged civic responsibility, and promoted a culture of service and community engagement among students. 

Beyond its immediate impact, the initiative promoted active citizenship, community engagement, and social responsibility, reflecting IEEE’s commitment to serving society and creating positive change through collective action.
