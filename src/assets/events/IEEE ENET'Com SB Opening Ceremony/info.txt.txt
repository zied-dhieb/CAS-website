Under the motto "New Year, New Spirit" the IEEE ENET’Com Student Branch, in collaboration with its IEEE CS, IAS, EMBS, IES-PES, RAS, CASS Chapters and WIE Affinity Group, launched the new academic year with its Opening Ceremony on September 13, 2026, at the Youth Center of Sakiet Ezzit. Supported by the active involvement of the STEP 3.0 Prep Team, the event brought together new members, officers, volunteers, and guests for a full day combining IEEE discovery, networking, team building, interactive activities, and community engagement.

The ceremony began with an opening address and an overview of the day’s program, followed by contributions from invited guests, including the President of the Sakiet Ezzit Youth Center. The Student Branch Advisor, Mr. Mohamed Neji, also took the stage to welcome the new members, share his experience, and provide valuable advice, feedback, and guidance to help them make the most of their IEEE journey. The IEEE ENET’Com SB Executive Committee then introduced the Student Branch, its mission, vision, and objectives for the new mandate. Each Chapter and Affinity Group subsequently presented its board, scope, activities, upcoming projects, and opportunities for involvement. A musical break helped create a relaxed atmosphere and encouraged participants to interact and connect.

The program continued with a dedicated Booth Tour, where officers and active members presented their units, events, technical projects, and IEEE experiences. In parallel, participants joined a team-based Bingo Challenge, which encouraged them to visit the different booths and collect information about IEEE, IEEE ENET’Com SB, its units, and CSTAM. Additional games and interactive activities allowed experienced members to share lessons from initiatives such as TSYP, annual congresses, and technical projects. The journey concluded with a Catch the Flag challenge, where teams tested the knowledge they had gained throughout the day.
Through this combination of technical awareness, community interaction, teamwork, and gamified learning, the Opening Ceremony helped new members better understand IEEE ENET’Com SB, discover its different opportunities, and build stronger connections within the community.
