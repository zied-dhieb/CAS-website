From self-doubt to self-belief: owning your potential.
As part of the IEEE Day celebrations, IEEE ENET’Com Student Branch, in collaboration with IEEE CASS ENET’Com Student Branch Chapter, hosted an online session entitled “From Impostor to Posture: Building Authentic Confidence”, led by Ghassen Medhioub, Senior Consultant specializing in Programme Design and Implementation, Entrepreneurship, Innovation, and Capacity Building.

Going beyond the technical side of engineering, the session explored a challenge many students and young professionals face: learning to believe in their abilities, recognize their achievements, and overcome the self-doubt that can hold them back. 
Through the theme of building authentic confidence, participants were invited to rethink their relationship with failure, success, and personal growth, while discovering how to move from questioning their worth to owning their potential. 

More than a discussion about confidence, the session encouraged participants to embrace their individuality, acknowledge their strengths, and develop a professional posture that reflects who they truly are. It served as a reminder that authentic confidence does not mean having all the answers, but having the courage to learn, grow, and step forward despite uncertainty.

Through this initiative, IEEE ENET’Com SB and IEEE CASS ENET’Com SBC reaffirmed the importance of nurturing not only technical excellence but also the personal and professional skills that empower future engineers to grow with confidence and purpose.
